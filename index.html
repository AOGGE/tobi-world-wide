How to spot a scam

  • Real admissions and scholarships never ask you to pay an agent or to send money to a personal account.
  • Check every admission list on the school's own portal and on JAMB CAPS.
  • Never share your JAMB or school portal password, your BVN or your card PIN.
  • If it sounds too good to be true, it is. Ask on the group before you pay.

Exam stress tips

          
  • Study in short blocks: 25 minutes, then a 5 minute break. Stand up and drink water.
  • Sleep matters more than one more late night. Try to sleep before midnight in exam week.
  • Do past questions. They show you how lecturers ask.
  • Eat something before an exam, and reach the hall early so you are calm.
  • If a question blocks you, skip it and come back. Do not let one question steal your time.
  • Talk to a friend, a course mate or someone you trust. Carrying stress alone makes it heavier.

          
You are not alone. This corner gives general tips only and is not medical advice. If you feel unsafe, hopeless or want to harm yourself, tell someone you trust right now and go to your school counselling unit or health centre. Open the Emergency tab for numbers.

Questions students ask

      

        
Is this free?
Yes. Reading the news, the guides and using the tools costs nothing.

        
How do I see news for my school?
Type your university in the "Your school" box in the news section. Posts for your school appear first, followed by guides for every student.

        
How do I know the information is correct?
We link to official sources where we can. Always confirm deadlines and fees on the official website of your school or the provider before you act.

        
My university is not in the list.
Choose "My university is not listed" when you register and type its name. You can also tell us on the WhatsApp channel and we will add it.

        
Where do you post updates first?
On the WhatsApp channel. Join it so you do not miss a deadline.
